# Nexus visual identity

The official Nexus mark is the violet orb with two soft white vertical eyes on a midnight background.

- Source artwork: `assets/branding/nexus-logo.svg`
- Primary background: `#090B1E`
- Orb palette: saturated violet through purple, with a soft lavender lower edge
- Eyes: white, softly rounded vertical capsules

Use the mark consistently for Nexus OS, Nexus Browser, the installer, recovery tools, and other first-party Nexus products. Keep the orb proportions and eye placement intact. Do not add letters inside the orb, redraw it as a generic lettermark, or recolor it for individual products.

The kernel desktop currently draws a framebuffer-native version for the startup splash, taskbar, app launcher, and Nexus Browser welcome surface. This avoids requiring an SVG or raster image decoder in the early boot environment. The SVG remains the scalable source artwork for documentation and future image-capable components.
