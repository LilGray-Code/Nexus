NEXUS APPLICATION INDEX
-----------------------

KINETIC.ELF
  Demonstrates the Nexus display, input, clock and framebuffer syscalls.

GAMEPAD.ELF
  Demonstrates bounded USB HID controller polling, buttons and axes.

AUDIO.ELF
  Demonstrates one bounded AC'97 48 kHz stereo PCM submission at a time.

PIE.ELF
  Demonstrates the static-PIE loader and symbol-free relative relocations.

To build your own native app, use kernel/include/nexus/app.h and run:
  make -C sdk CC=gcc
\nARCADE.ELF
  Play Nexus Breakout with keyboard arrows or a supported USB HID gamepad.
